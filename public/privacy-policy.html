Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026
Before you rely on this: this policy was drafted by Claude to describe, as accurately as possible, the data Kaf Musician Connect actually collects and how it's handled. It is a starting draft, not legal advice, and Claude is not a lawyer. Have it reviewed by a lawyer familiar with privacy law in the regions where your users live (for example GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California) before you publish it or submit your app for review — those laws may require additional disclosures, rights, or consent mechanisms not covered here.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Kaf Musician Connect ("we," "us," "the app") collects, uses, and stores information when you use our website or mobile app to find, book, or provide musician, singer, MC, or DJ services, or to rent or list event equipment.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address, password (stored as a salted, one-way cryptographic hash — we never store or can see your actual password), name, and phone number.
  • Your account role (client, musician, equipment owner, or admin).

Musician profile information

If you create a musician profile, we additionally collect:

  • Stage name, instruments, genres, bio, hourly rate, city and state.
  • Optional details you choose to add: photos, a video link, whether you're available for emergency/last-minute bookings, self-reported insurance status, and a designated backup musician.
  • ID verification status. This is currently a self-service demo toggle and does not involve a real identity check or the collection of an actual ID document.

Equipment owner & listing information

  • Business name, bio, city and state for equipment owner profiles.
  • Equipment listings: category, title, description, photos, daily rate, and security deposit amount.

Bookings, rentals, and related activity

  • Booking details: event date/time, duration, location, offered and agreed rate, status, and whether it's marked emergency or group.
  • Rental details: rental dates, daily rate, number of days, fees, and deposit.
  • Reviews and star ratings you leave on completed bookings.
  • Favorites (musicians or listings you save).
  • In-app notifications generated by activity on your account.
  • Reports or disputes you file, or that are filed about your account, including the reason and any resolution notes.

Session & technical information

  • A session token stored in an HttpOnly cookie, used to keep you signed in. It isn't readable by page scripts and is only sent back to our own server.
  • Standard web server request information (such as the fact a request was made and when) needed to operate the site.

What we don't currently collect or do

  • We do not currently process real payments. Fees, rates, and deposits are calculated and stored so you can see what a booking would cost, but no payment processor is connected and no money moves through the app yet.
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks, ad trackers, or analytics/tracking pixels.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to keep you signed in.
  • To operate the core features you use: search, bookings, rentals, reviews, favorites, notifications, and Trust & Safety reports/disputes.
  • To show your musician or equipment listing to prospective clients, if you choose to create one.
  • To communicate with you about your account and activity (in-app notifications).
  • To send you a password reset email if you request one — this is the only case where we email you directly rather than notifying you inside the app.
  • To respond to reports or disputes and, where necessary, to suspend or reinstate accounts that violate our terms.

Password reset emails

If you use the "Forgot password?" link, we send a one-time reset link to the email on your account through a transactional email provider (Resend). That provider processes your email address and the message content solely to deliver that email on our behalf, under its own provider-level privacy and security practices — it does not independently market to you or use your address for any other purpose. Reset links expire after one hour and can only be used once.

Who can see your information

Musician and equipment listing profiles (stage name/business name, bio, rates, city/state, photos, reviews) are shown publicly within the app so clients can find and evaluate them. Booking and rental details are visible to the two parties involved in that booking or rental, and to admins for the purpose of resolving disputes or reports. Your password is never visible to anyone, including us — only its salted hash is stored.

Data retention

We keep your account and activity data for as long as your account is active, so the app can function (booking history, reviews, etc.).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: go to Account in the navigation bar, then Delete my account under "Danger zone," and confirm with your password. Deleting your account immediately removes your public profile and listings, your favorites, and your notifications, and signs you out on every device.

A small amount of information tied to bookings, rentals, disputes, or reports may be kept in anonymized form (your name replaced with "Deleted User") so the other party's transaction history and any open disputes remain intact — no personally identifying information (your email, phone number, or profile details) is kept once your account is deleted.

Data security

Passwords are hashed using scrypt with a per-user salt before storage. Session identifiers are stored in HttpOnly cookies. Data is stored in a database on our hosting provider's infrastructure. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • You can edit most of your profile information at any time from within the app.
  • You can remove a musician or equipment listing you've created.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Kaf Musician Connect is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law, if higher). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be announced in the app.
