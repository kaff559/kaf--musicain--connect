Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Kaf Musician Connect ("we," "us," "the app") collects, uses, and stores information when you use our website or mobile app to find, book, or provide musician, singer, MC, or DJ services, or to rent or list event equipment.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address, password (stored as a salted, one-way cryptographic hash — we never store or can see your actual password), name, phone number, and date of birth.
  • Your account role (client, musician, equipment owner, or admin).
  • If you sign up under 18: a parent or guardian's name and email address, collected solely to request their approval of your account. See "Accounts for users under 18" below.

Musician profile information

If you create a musician profile, we additionally collect:

  • Stage name, instruments, genres, bio, hourly rate, city and state.
  • Optional details you choose to add: photos, a photo gallery, a video link, skill level per instrument/role, years of experience, whether you read chord charts/Nashville numbers/sheet music, whether you own equipment or can lead rehearsals, weekly availability and blackout dates, paid/volunteer compensation preference, travel radius, references, whether you're available for emergency/last-minute bookings, self-reported insurance status, and a designated backup musician.
  • ID verification status. This is currently a self-service demo toggle and does not involve a real identity check or the collection of an actual ID document.

Equipment owner & listing information

  • Business name, bio, city and state for equipment owner profiles.
  • Equipment listings: category, title, description, photos, daily rate, and security deposit amount.

Bookings, rentals, and related activity

  • Booking details: event date/time, duration, location, offered and agreed rate, status, and whether it's marked emergency or group.
  • Rental details: rental dates, daily rate, number of days, fees, and deposit.
  • Job postings and responses to them.
  • Reviews and star ratings you leave on completed bookings.
  • Favorites (musicians or listings you save).
  • In-app notifications generated by activity on your account.
  • Reports or disputes you file, or that are filed about your account, including the reason and any resolution notes.

Payment information

Payments are collected and processed directly by PayPal — whether you pay with a PayPal balance/bank account, a debit/credit card through PayPal's guest checkout, or Apple Pay, we never see or store your full card number, PayPal login credentials, or Apple Pay details (for an Apple Pay payment, PayPal and Apple handle that exchange directly; we only ever receive a one-time payment token). We do store the booking or rental amount, the payment's status (for example, authorized, charged, refunded, or paid out), and an internal order/authorization/capture reference id PayPal gives us for that payment. If you create a musician or equipment-owner profile and add a payout email so you can be paid, that's the PayPal account email address you give us directly — we send payouts to it through PayPal's Payouts service but don't otherwise see into that PayPal account. See PayPal's own privacy policy for how they handle information processed on their end.

Session & technical information

  • A session token stored in an HttpOnly cookie, used to keep you signed in. It isn't readable by page scripts and is only sent back to our own server.
  • Standard web server request information (such as the fact a request was made and when) needed to operate the site.

What we don't currently collect or do

  • We do not use third-party advertising networks, ad trackers, or analytics/tracking pixels.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to keep you signed in.
  • To operate the core features you use: search, bookings, rentals, job postings, reviews, favorites, notifications, and Trust & Safety reports/disputes.
  • To show your musician or equipment listing to prospective clients, if you choose to create one.
  • To communicate with you about your account and activity (in-app notifications).
  • To send you a password reset email if you request one, or — for an account signed up under 18 — a one-time email to the parent/guardian you listed, asking them to approve the account. These are the only cases where we email you (or your guardian) directly rather than notifying you inside the app.
  • To respond to reports or disputes and, where necessary, to suspend or reinstate accounts that violate our terms.

Accounts for users under 18

If you sign up and tell us you're under 18, we ask for a parent or guardian's name and email address and send them a one-time link to approve your account, as described in our Terms of Service. Until they approve, the account can browse the app but can't publish a profile, post or apply to jobs, or make a booking or rental. We use the guardian's email only for that approval request and directly related account notices — not for marketing, and not shared with other users. The approval link itself expires after 14 days.

Password reset & parental-approval emails

If you use the "Forgot password?" link, or if you sign up under 18 and we need to reach your parent/guardian for approval, we send that one-time link through a transactional email provider (Resend). That provider processes the recipient's email address and the message content solely to deliver that email on our behalf, under its own provider-level privacy and security practices — it does not independently market to them or use the address for any other purpose. Password reset links expire after one hour; parental-approval links expire after 14 days. Each can only be used once.

Who can see your information

Musician and equipment listing profiles (stage name/business name, bio, rates, city/state, photos, reviews) are shown publicly within the app so clients can find and evaluate them. References you add to a musician profile are shown only to logged-in clients, not to the general public. Booking and rental details are visible to the two parties involved in that booking or rental, and to admins for the purpose of resolving disputes or reports. Your password is never visible to anyone, including us — only its salted hash is stored.

Data retention

We keep your account and activity data for as long as your account is active, so the app can function (booking history, reviews, etc.).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: go to Account in the navigation bar, then Delete my account under "Danger zone," and confirm with your password. Deleting your account immediately removes your public profile and listings, your favorites, and your notifications, and signs you out on every device.

A small amount of information tied to bookings, rentals, disputes, or reports may be kept in anonymized form (your name replaced with "Deleted User") so the other party's transaction history and any open disputes remain intact — no personally identifying information (your email, phone number, date of birth, or profile details) is kept once your account is deleted.

Data security

Passwords are hashed using scrypt with a per-user salt before storage. Session identifiers are stored in HttpOnly cookies. Data is stored in a database on our hosting provider's infrastructure. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • You can edit most of your profile information at any time from within the app.
  • You can remove a musician or equipment listing you've created.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Kaf Musician Connect allows accounts for users under 18 only with a parent or guardian's approval, as described above — see "Accounts for users under 18." We don't knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 outside of that parent/guardian-approval flow itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be announced in the app.
