# Store listing copy — Kaf Musician Connect

Copy-paste starting points for the Google Play Console and App Store Connect
listing forms. Character limits are noted next to each field — trim if your
final app name or wording pushes it over. Edit freely; this is a draft, not
final marketing copy.

## App name

**Kaf Musician Connect**

## Google Play

**Short description** (max 80 characters)
```
Book musicians, singers, MCs & DJs, or rent event equipment. All in one app.
```
(78 characters)

**Full description** (max 4000 characters)
```
Kaf Musician Connect is a marketplace that connects musicians, singers,
MCs, and DJs with the people who want to hire them — plus a peer-to-peer
marketplace for renting event equipment like speakers, microphones, drum
kits, DJ gear, and lighting.

FOR CLIENTS
• Search by instrument, genre, city, or budget — try natural language like
  "gospel piano player under $100 an hour"
• View real profiles: bio, hourly rate, genres, media, and reviews from
  past clients
• Send a booking request with your event date, time, and location
• Message back and forth with counter-offers until you agree on a rate
• Leave a star rating and review after your event
• Need gear instead of (or in addition to) a performer? Browse and rent
  speakers, mics, drum kits, DJ equipment, and lighting from local owners

FOR MUSICIANS, SINGERS, MCs & DJs
• Build a profile: stage name, instruments, genres, hourly rate, bio, and
  media
• Mark yourself available for emergency or last-minute bookings
• Accept, decline, or counter-offer incoming booking requests
• Track upcoming and past bookings in one dashboard
• Build your reputation with client reviews

FOR EQUIPMENT OWNERS
• List your gear with photos, a daily rate, and a refundable security
  deposit
• Manage incoming rental requests and track rental history

TRUST & SAFETY
• Real accounts with secure password protection
• Report or file a dispute on any booking or rental
• Delete your account and data at any time from inside the app

Whether you're planning a wedding, a party, a church service, or a show —
or you're the musician, MC, DJ, or gear owner other people are searching
for — Kaf Musician Connect brings both sides of the event together in one
place.
```

**App category:** Events, or Music & Audio (pick whichever your Play
Console offers that fits closest — both are reasonable)

**Tags/keywords to consider:** musician booking, DJ booking, live music,
event musician, equipment rental, MC booking, wedding musician, church
musician

## Apple App Store

**Subtitle** (max 30 characters — shows under the app name)
```
Book musicians & rent gear
```
(27 characters)

**Promotional text** (max 170 characters — can be updated anytime without
a new app version)
```
Find and book musicians, singers, MCs & DJs for your next event, or rent
speakers, mics, and DJ gear from local owners. All in one app.
```

**Description** (max 4000 characters — same content as the Google Play
full description above works fine here too; Apple doesn't require
different wording)

**Keywords** (max 100 characters, comma-separated, no spaces after commas
— Apple strips duplicates of words already in your app name/subtitle)
```
musician,booking,DJ,MC,singer,live music,event,wedding,equipment rental,gear,church
```

**Category:** Primary: Music. Secondary (optional): Business or Lifestyle.

## Notes

- Neither store listing should claim in-app payment processing yet — the
  app calculates and displays fees/totals, but no payment processor is
  wired up (see `README.md`). If that changes, update the copy to mention
  it.
- Both stores will ask for your privacy policy URL — that's
  `https://YOUR-LIVE-DOMAIN/privacy-policy.html` once deployed.
- Screenshots: both stores require at least 2 (Play) or a device-specific
  set (Apple, per required screen sizes). Good candidates from this app:
  the home search page, a musician's profile, a booking request/dashboard
  view, and the equipment rental browse page.
